### 🧭 What I do

Together with Nelina I run **NeuKo know-how**, a small company on the Danish west coast.
We build **websites and software your customers use to enquire and book – in Danish, German and English.**

- 🌐 **Websites that bring enquiries** – not just visitors
- 🛒 **Online booking and web shops**
- ⚙️ **Processes that run by themselves** – automation, including AI where it saves real work
- 🤝 **Customer relations and portals** – CRM and a customer portal for documents, decisions and approvals

Our focus is the German–Danish bridge: businesses with many German guests or customers,
currently mostly in tourism around Blåvand, Vejers and Henne.

I'm the developer in the team – from analysis and architecture to deployment and running the systems.
